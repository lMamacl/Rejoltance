Sound effects go in this folder. Each sound has 3 variants (one is picked at random on every play):

  attack  buff  burn  cardDrawn  damageTaken  debuff  defeat  endTurn  heal
  heroHit  mark  silence  spellCast  stun  unitDeath  unitPlayed  victory

  e.g.  attack_1.wav  attack_2.wav  attack_3.wav  ...  (17 sounds x 3 = 51 files)

Missing or broken files are skipped silently. To use .ogg/.mp3 instead of .wav, change SFX_EXT at the top of sfx.js.

The 51 .wav files here were generated by  python3 tools/make_sfx.py  (needs numpy). Re-run it after editing the recipes to regenerate them,
or overwrite any file with your own recording (16-bit PCM WAV works everywhere).
